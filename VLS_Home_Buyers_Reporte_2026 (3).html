Personal credit — what the statements show
  • Helps
    Clean payment record. No past-due amounts or late fees on any of the 28 statements. Fact
  • Helps
    Pay-in-full habit since Aug 2025 eliminated card interest ($0 in 2026), and Chase raised the line twice. Fact
  • Hurts
    Balances swing around the statement date. Large balances build (up to 80% in Oct-24) and are paid down after the statement closes. Issuers generally report the statement balance, so reported utilization can look high even when the bill is paid in full. Fact
  • Hurts

  • Watch
